# Visual Identity & Design System

## Core Aesthetic
The visual identity is an "Editorial Art-Zine." It should feel alternative, tactile, confident, and dark (but not a generic black void)[cite: 5]. The design must prioritize art direction, composition, intentional spacing, and strong visual hierarchy over flashy web tricks[cite: 6].

## Typography
- **Primary (Headings):** Brutalist, tightly tracked sans-serif (inspired by the "LINES" graphic).
- **Secondary (Body/Poetry):** A mix of clean readability and occasional pixelated/bitmap or classic serif accents (inspired by the "NATURE" and "Panchtantra Rasaayan" graphics). 
- **Treatment:** Strong typographic rhythm. Embrace deliberate asymmetry and overlapping elements[cite: 5].

## Color Palette
- **Base:** High-contrast pitch black and stark white.
- **Accents:** 
  - **Cyan/Mint:** Pulled directly from `Band Icon.PNG`[cite: 2].
  - **Washed-out Pinks & Sky Blues:** Nostalgic, lomography tones pulled from the "Panchtantra Rasaayan" cover and the pink building polaroid.

## Layout Philosophy
- **Editorial over Corporate:** Use strict, invisible grids but allow elements (images, text) to break out of them, creating a raw, physical zine feeling[cite: 5].
- **Imagery:** Large, imposing imagery[cite: 5]. Photography should feature dithered/halftone treatments (like the "US AND THEM" graphic) or soft-focus film grain (like the pink university polaroid).
- **Animation:** Restrained. No bouncy easing. Use hard cuts, subtle noise overlays, or stark, abrupt reveals[cite: 5].

## Component Guidelines
- **Cards:** No soft drop shadows or excessive rounded corners[cite: 6]. 
- **Navigation:** Minimalist, perhaps fixed to a side or integrated directly into the hero artwork.
